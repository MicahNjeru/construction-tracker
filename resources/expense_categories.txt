# Expense Categories for Construction Project Tracker
# Format: key|name|description
# Lines starting with # are comments

transport | Material Transport | Cost of transporting materials from supplier to construction site
licenses | Government Licenses & Permits | Fees for construction permits, licenses, and regulatory approvals
transaction_fees | Transaction Charges | Bank fees, M-Pesa charges, and payment processing fees
utilities | Utilities | Electricity, water, and other utility costs during construction
equipment_rental | Equipment Rental | Rental fees for machinery, tools, and equipment
tool_purchase | Tool Purchase | Tool purchase, reusable item purchase
professional_services | Professional Services | Fees for architects, engineers, surveyors, and consultants
insurance | Insurance | Construction insurance, liability coverage, and worker compensation
waste_disposal | Waste Disposal | Cost of removing construction debris and waste management
security | Security | Security guards, fencing, and site protection
office_supplies | Office & Admin Supplies | Stationery, printing, administrative materials
inspection_fees | Inspection Fees | Fees for mandatory inspections and quality assurance tests
contingency | Contingency | Unexpected expenses and miscellaneous costs
food_accommodation | Food & Accommodation | Meals and accommodation for workers when applicable
communication | Communication | Phone bills, internet, and communication expenses
fuel | Fuel & Vehicle Maintenance | Fuel costs and vehicle maintenance for project vehicles
legal_fees | Legal Fees | Legal consultation and documentation fees
taxes | Taxes & Levies | Government taxes and local authority levies
advertising | Marketing & Advertising | Costs for project promotion and signage
training | Training & Safety | Worker training and safety equipment costs
other | Other Expenses | Miscellaneous expenses not covered by other categories
